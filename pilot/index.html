We are checking clarity and usability. This session does not measure your employability or establish that an AI coach improves learning.

What participation involves

You will try one assigned form with four uncoached JavaScript debugging tasks. The current protocol allows 12 minutes per task, with the operator managing time. You can skip a task or stop at any time.

No name, participant email, employer, age or identity proof is requested. Your source, reasoning and ratings stay in your browser until you choose to export. Exporting creates a file; it does not email or automatically send it to us.

Do not include secrets or personal information in notes. Before handing over any export, agree with the operator how they will store it and when they will delete it. Removing a browser draft does not remove files you previously exported or shared.

Questions or deletion requests for a shared copy: . Use your random calibration code when contacting the operator.

The form is a development assessment, not a certification or job application. Source reviewers and anyone who has seen both forms can still try the workflow, but their observations are excluded from independent task-difficulty summaries.

Demo records are always marked as technical data and cannot count as human calibration results.
